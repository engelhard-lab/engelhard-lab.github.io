Enhancing, deploying, and validating the Duke Predictive Model of Adolescent Mental Health (Duke-PMA), which uses accessible measures such as sleep to identify youth at high risk for psychiatric illness, in rural primary care clinics. This project is part of the [IMPACT-MH consortium](https://impact-mh.org/awardees/pma/).
