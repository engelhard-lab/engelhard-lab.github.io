Characterizing longitudinal disease trajectories in systemic lupus erythematosus to identify subgroups of patients with distinct disease courses. We examine the relative importance of objective clinical findings, such as laboratory measures, versus self-reported features in shaping these trajectories, and how each contributes to predicting disease flares.
