A national initiative to bridge the gap between AI research and its use in care for older adults and people with Alzheimer's disease and related dementias, through pilot funding, shared data science infrastructure, and resources for responsible evaluation and governance of AI tools. Learn more on the [Duke AITC-AIGE website](https://aitc.duke.edu).
