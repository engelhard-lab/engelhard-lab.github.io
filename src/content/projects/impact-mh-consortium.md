A national NIMH consortium that uses behavioral measures and computational methods to define clinical signatures for individual-level prediction and clinical decision-making in mental disorders. Our project, the Predictive Model of Adolescent Mental Health (PMA), is one of the consortium's research partner awards. Learn more on the [IMPACT-MH website](https://impact-mh.org/).
