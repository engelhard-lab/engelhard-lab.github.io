Many of the conditions we study manifest over long periods of time, so typical follow-up times are much shorter than typical times to diagnosis, leading to high rates of right-censoring; event times are also often affected by systemic biases. We develop methods for long-horizon prediction that boost performance, mitigate biases arising from the censoring and event distributions, and ensure predictions are equitable between demographic subgroups. This work includes neural mixture cure models, discrete failure time models, and metrics and methods that promote fairness in time to event settings.
