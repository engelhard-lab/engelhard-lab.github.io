We aim to develop clinical decision support that elicits patient preferences in addition to reasoning about likely clinical trajectories, promoting decisions that are both clinically optimal and preference-concordant. We are developing LLM-based methods to elicit information about preferences efficiently and to identify the specific text that informs beliefs about them, along with mixed modeling approaches for heterogeneous preference data, for example to create a “digital twin” that captures a specific patient's preferences about treatment.
