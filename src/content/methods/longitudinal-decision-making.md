Building on our work in longitudinal risk prediction, we are turning our attention toward longitudinal decision-making for slowly evolving conditions, using large, multi-modal longitudinal datasets. This requires models that continually monitor and update their beliefs about the likelihood of specific events, flexibly integrate information across modalities over time, and reason about how those beliefs might change as additional information is acquired. Our goal is interpretable, AI-based clinical decision support that directly supports decision-making through context-sensitive recommendations, rather than indirectly through risk predictions alone.
