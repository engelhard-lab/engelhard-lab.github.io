As clinical decision support tools proliferate, it will be important for AI-based systems to carefully limit their requests for provider attention and optimize their selection of what information to present. We view these tools as agents that must negotiate for provider attention based on risk predictions together with clinical knowledge accessed through retrieval. Early work includes a multi-LLM-agent system that reasons about adverse drug events from clinical databases and document collections, and we intend to develop multi-agent systems that collaborate to support decision-making for medically and psychiatrically complex patients.
