**A note to prospective PhD students:** I work with PhD students in our [Biostatistics](https://biostat.duke.edu/education-and-training/biostatistics-phd), [CBB](https://medschool.duke.edu/education/biomedical-phd-programs/computational-biology-and-bioinformatics-program), and [ECE](https://ece.duke.edu/admissions/phd/) programs, and will likely recruit 1–2 students for Fall 2027. Please apply to the program that best suits your interests and mention me in your application so I'll know to review it. I may not respond to your email, but this does not mean I'm not interested in your application. Please be aware that the Biostatistics and CBB programs admit by committee, and students complete rotations rather than associating with an advisor on admission.
